What runs behind the Grafana-to-Perses converter's address

The converter is an optional part of the openshift-coo chart (converter.enabled). It is one pod behind one Route. This page shows the path a request takes from a browser to the conversion engine, where the OpenShift login sits on that path, and what leaves the pod.

Every label is read from charts/openshift-coo/templates/60-converter.yaml and from the objects on the lab (CRC 4.22.7, namespace platform-tools), measured in docs/evidence/crc/15-converter.txt.
